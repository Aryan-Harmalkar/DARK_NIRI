# Session: One Piece Straw Hat Crew Animated Wallpaper Theme

**Date:** 2026-09-18 12:29:00
**Category:** new-features

## Modifications
- **Normalized Character Assets**: Retrieved full-body transparent PNG renders for all 10 Straw Hat Pirates (Luffy, Zoro, Nami, Usopp, Sanji, Chopper, Robin, Franky, Brook, Jinbe). Cropped each figure to its non-transparent bounding box and scaled/centered onto a standardized 800×1000 canvas with uniform padding to ensure no cut-off heads or feet.
- **Image Storage**: Exported normalized transparent PNGs to both `~/Downloads/straw_hats/` and the theme's local `images/` directory.
- **One Piece Animated Wallpaper (`one-piece-crew`)**: Built a single self-contained HTML/CSS/JS wallpaper file (1920×1080 responsive canvas) with:
  - **Left Info Panel**: `PREV` and `NEXT` pill navigation buttons, counter (`01 / 10`), two-line character title (Name + Epithet), role badge, official stats (Bounty, Height, Birthday), and biographical summary.
  - **Autoplay Controls**: Toggable `PAUSE TIMER` / `RESUME TIMER` button, numeric interval selector (`Every [N] seconds`, default 10s), and live countdown ticker (`Next member in Xs`).
  - **Right Side Stage**: Displays the active character's transparent figure layered in front of an oversized, low-opacity rotated watermark displaying their epithet and full name, complete with smooth cross-fade, slide animations, and gentle idle breathing.
  - **Top-Right Telemetry**: Circular live analog/digital clock badge with rotating hands and date, paired with a Grand Line weather widget.
  - **Order of Succession**: Cycles through the crew strictly in canonical join order: Luffy → Zoro → Nami → Usopp → Sanji → Chopper → Robin → Franky → Brook → Jinbe.
- **Integration**: Created `wallpaper.json` manifest for instant discovery in QuickShell Settings and `wallpaperctl`. Also provided a standalone copy at `~/Downloads/one_piece_wallpaper.html`.
