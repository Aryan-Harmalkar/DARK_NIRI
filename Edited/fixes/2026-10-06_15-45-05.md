# Session: Prevent Mouse Warping During Workspace Scrolling

**Date:** 2026-10-06 15:45:05
**Category:** fixes

## Modifications
- Removed compositor-level `focus-monitor` calls from `scrollWorkspaces()` and `switchToWorkspace()` in `quickshell/components/Workspaces.qml`. In Niri, `focus-monitor` teleports the mouse cursor to the center of the monitor by default, which was moving the cursor away from the top bar during wheel scrolling.
- Retained clean workspace switching via `niri msg action focus-workspace-down`, `focus-workspace-up`, and `focus-workspace <idx>`, which cycle workspaces without displacing the mouse pointer.
- Added wheel delta accumulation threshold (`accumulatedDelta >= 60`) in `MouseArea` to ensure smooth, controlled, and continuous scroll transitions without jitter.
- Reloaded Quickshell via `quickshell/reload-shell.sh`.
- Updated documentation in `docs/complex/QUICKSHELL.md`.
