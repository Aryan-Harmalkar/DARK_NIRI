# Session: Fix Missing Window Control Buttons (Close, Minimize, Maximize)

**Date:** 2026-09-19 20:12:04
**Category:** fixes

## Modifications
- Identified that `org.gnome.desktop.wm.preferences button-layout` and `org.cinnamon.desktop.wm.preferences button-layout` GSettings keys were empty (`''`), causing GTK, GNOME, Cinnamon, and Wayland client-side decorated apps to suppress all titlebar control buttons.
- Configured GSettings `button-layout` to `'appmenu:minimize,maximize,close'` live and added the configuration to `niri/startup.sh` and `deploy.sh` for persistence across boots and deployments.
- Added standard window closing keybindings (`Mod+Q` and `Alt+F4`) alongside existing `Alt+F` in `niri/config.kdl` and reloaded Niri configuration live.
- Updated documentation across `README.md`, `keybindings-docs.md`, `docs/complex/KEYBINDS.md`, and `docs/simple/KEYBOARD_SHORTCUTS.md`.
